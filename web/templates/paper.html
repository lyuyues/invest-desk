Market-wide portfolio (scans all US stocks)

After each close, scans all US-listed stocks with market cap ≥ $1B (excluding OTC), using the DCF fair value. Financials, real estate and utilities, plus foreign companies that don’t report in USD, are excluded from the universe: free-cash-flow DCF doesn’t suit them and the fair values come out absurd. Stocks meeting the entry conditions fill open slots from highest to lowest upside, each with 1/N of the capital; holdings that meet the exit conditions are sold. Signals are generated at the close and filled at the next trading day’s close. Only valuation / market-cap conditions and stop-loss / take-profit conditions can be used ({{ port_strats|length }} strategies available; on the backtest page, “Copy & edit” makes new ones).
